Meta Launches Muse Agent for Small Businesses

Meta’s Muse for Small Business connects Facebook and Instagram business accounts with tools including Canva, Figma, and Slack to help automate small-business workflows, while requiring approval before publishing, sending, or spending.

A narrower route into agentic software

Meta has launched Muse for Small Business, designed to connect Facebook and Instagram business accounts with tools including Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe and Zoom. The product targets businesses that often lack dedicated analysts, designers or operations staff but already use Meta’s advertising and social platforms.

Muse’s importance lies in the workflow it tries to assemble. Instead of answering a question about campaign performance, it can help analyze sales, campaigns and social activity, prepare plans or creative work, and organize follow-up tasks across connected applications. Meta says nothing is published, sent or spent without the user’s approval.

The integrations also show Meta’s distribution advantage. Facebook and Instagram provide a large source of first-party business data, while compatibility with Canva, Figma, Slack and other services lets Meta reach beyond its own products. If the agent becomes useful, Meta can make its social platforms the starting point for a broader business operating layer without owning every application in the workflow.

The main constraint is trust. Small businesses may be more willing than large enterprises to experiment, but they are also less able to absorb an incorrect recommendation, a misread metric or an accidental action. Data access and approval controls will therefore matter as much as the model’s analytical ability.

Why it matters

This is a meaningful agent story because it connects a high-volume data source to everyday execution tools. Meta is not merely adding another assistant; it is testing whether its platform can become the command center for small-business marketing and operations.

Uncle Cat take: Muse’s edge is the Facebook–Instagram data moat, but its approval requirement also shows that Meta has not delegated final business actions to the agent.
