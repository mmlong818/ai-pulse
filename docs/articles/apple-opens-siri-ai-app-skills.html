Apple opens Siri AI app skills to third-party developers

Apple’s latest developer beta lets apps expose richer actions to Siri AI, potentially turning the assistant into a system-wide interface for software.

Siri becomes an app platform

Apple has opened Siri AI integrations to all developers through the iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, macOS 27.3 and visionOS 27.3 developer betas. The new preview allows apps to expose capabilities that users can invoke conversationally through Siri. Apple says developers have already built integrations for directions, to-do lists, messaging and audiobook discovery.

The change is more consequential than a conventional voice shortcut. Apple describes Siri AI as a system-level assistant that can understand requests and reach into apps, while developers provide the actions and data needed to complete them. The company says the feature supports English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish at launch, with Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese planned next.

Access is not universal. Siri AI requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible device and a supported language. Apple says the feature is currently unavailable in the European Union on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS. Server-side Apple Intelligence features are also subject to daily usage limits that may vary with demand, request complexity and policy.

Why it matters

Apple is attempting to make the operating system, rather than an individual chatbot, the primary distribution layer for AI actions. If developers adopt the interface, users could discover and operate software without opening each app, increasing the value of Apple’s platform while giving developers a new route to engagement. The risk is fragmentation: regional restrictions, device requirements and usage caps could make the experience inconsistent, while app permissions and action quality will determine whether users trust Siri with real tasks.

Uncle Cat take

Apple has finally supplied the missing developer doorway, but the EU exclusion means Siri’s promised system-wide reach is still geographically incomplete.
