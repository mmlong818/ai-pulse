Baidu open-sources 280B-parameter ERNIE 5.5 Flash

Baidu releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview under Apache 2.0, giving developers global access to a fast MoE model with a million-token context.

What happened

Baidu has released the weights and supporting resources for ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview under the Apache 2.0 license. The model is available through Hugging Face and ModelScope, with deployment support for vLLM and SGLang. Its publication is the important event here: developers can download, run, fine-tune, and adapt the model rather than merely access a hosted preview.

Baidu describes the system as a 280-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with roughly 14 billion parameters activated per token. It supports a context window of up to one million tokens and is positioned for coding, 3D modelling, knowledge work, and content creation. QbitAI reports that the model reached a CodeArena WebDev score of 1668, placing it second among open models in that ranking at publication.

Why it matters

The release adds a major Chinese model to the globally accessible open-weight supply. The combination of a large total parameter count, relatively small active computation, long context, and permissive licensing gives researchers more room to test local deployment and domain adaptation without accepting a proprietary API’s usage boundaries.

The headline numbers still need independent replication. “Preview” status leaves questions about stability, serving cost, multilingual quality, and how the model behaves outside the publisher’s selected evaluations. The hardware burden also remains substantial despite the sparse architecture. Still, the licensing and downloadable artifacts make this more consequential than another API refresh: it gives the open ecosystem a new high-capability baseline from a Chinese lab during a period when model access and deployment sovereignty are becoming strategic concerns.
